## How the comparison clip was chosen

Not hand-picked. `tools/score_pipe_gap.py` scores 67 clips that have both a
pipeline render and a MINT render by hand-overlay alignment, and
`assets/pipe_gap.json` ranks them. `ego4d_c001_f869_ep00175026_o001` has the largest gap among
the 1024-wide sources (gap 0.094; rank 2 of 67 overall) and no missing
frames on either side (34 annotated hands, 24 pipeline /
24 MINT samples).

That score is an internal selection heuristic for picking a legible example.
It is **not** an accuracy metric and appears nowhere as a result. Quantitative
results come only from `eval/model_effect/benchmark/` on HOT3D and ARCTIC.
